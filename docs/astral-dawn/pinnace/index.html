Armoured Pinnace

40 tons · TL13 · ex-pirate salvage, badly damaged and patched · external docking clamps on the Astral Dawn’s hull — “a tiny SDB, and the crew effectively own her.”

What she is. Not a ship’s boat. She is a heavily armoured, heavily armed small craft taken intact from pirates, pressure-hardened well past the usual standard, and carrying an armoured G-Carrier in her own hold. In practice she is the Astral Dawn’s system defence boat and her assault transport at the same time — which is why deploying her costs the fighter squadron a pilot.

How she is used

Three doctrines are on record, and they differ in what reaches the ground rather than in what she does:

DoctrineLandsCharacter
Pinnace assaultFull squad Heavy armour and the pulse laser go in with the marines.
G-Carrier assaultSquad of eight The carrier drops from the pinnace and fights on its own mounted weapons. Best for surface work.
Combined armsSquad, split Pinnace delivers the carrier to proximity, then stands off and gives covering fire. Maximum force.

Standard deployment: pinnace to orbit or close proximity → G-Carrier descends with the marines aboard.

Unresolved — needs a referee ruling

These are conflicts between campaign records, not gaps I could fill by looking harder. Each one is a place where two documents disagree, so the number below is a choice somebody has to make.

  • Displacement. 40 tons in the fleet record, the combat reference and the handout text — but 22 tons in the ship record’s carried-craft list, and 60 tons in one map tooltip. 40 is used throughout this page.
  • Turret fit. The combat reference gives missile rack + pulse laser + sandcaster. One map tooltip instead reads “missile racks & sandcasters, turreted light beam”. The triple turret is used here.
  • Tech level. Campaign sources say TL13 consistently; the generic pinnace template she was built from is TL9. Her armour, speed and pressure rating all argue for TL13.
  • Armour rating. Described everywhere as “heavy”, nowhere as a number. She cannot be resolved in ship combat until this has a value.
  • Thrust and power plant. Not recorded anywhere for this variant.
