Deckplan — general arrangement

Forward, the drum — the Green Space, a hydroponic garden and the only part of the ship with a horizon. It is a cylinder standing on end, its axis running dorsal to ventral, so the garden decks are stacked broad and shallow and the whole section reads as a rectangle from alongside. A short neck joins it to the main fuselage, which carries the bridge, staterooms, barracks, the medical bay and the low-berth banks, with the drive section aft. The subcraft dock is beneath the fuselage in the standard position for the class; the armoured pinnace rides a cradle on top of the hull. Full deckplan is held under separate cover.

Section 2 • Cover and Concealment

What she is pretending to be

For most of her service the Astral Dawn sailed as an Oberlindes Lines subsidised liner working the Caladbolg–Collace run, with a manifest of thirty souls hired through a discretionary fund and no questions asked.

That cover no longer holds in full. Following the Senate vote at Collace the vessel was publicly reclassified as an armed merchant operating under JSI patrol contract. She is now a ship that admits to carrying guns while declining to say how many, which is a materially different kind of lie and in several respects a more useful one.

Concealment

Every mount is pop-up. Nothing protrudes until the order is given. The hull is adjustable at TL-15 and her external appearance can be altered between ports.

Emissions

The absorption grid returns DM −3 against detection. Armour was deliberately held at 1 — capital-grade plate registers as a density anomaly on any close scan.

Stowage

A concealed compartment returns DM −2 to sensors and −4 to any physical investigation. Contents are not itemised in this document.

The Potemkin village

Aft, near the launch bay, two staterooms are partitioned off from the rest of the ship by bulkhead and by access control. They are ordinary double-occupancy cabins — there is nothing special about them and normally the crew sleeps in them. Their purpose is that they can be emptied.

When the ship needs to look like what her papers say she is, she takes on paying travellers. They board, they are shown to staterooms, they are fed, and from inside that partition the vessel presents as an unremarkable liner. There are no indicators available to them that it is anything else. The section has been used in this way several times, and it defrays a great deal of suspicion for very little cost.

Currently berthed in the passenger section: Torin Sable and Dr. Meera Vasant, the designer and the implementer of the automated refuelling systems, professionally engaged for the Rift depot programme. How much either has been told about the ship they are riding is a command decision and is not recorded here.

The ion barbette drains power rather than breaching hull, and it ignores armour entirely; a solid hit strips a target's reserves and takes its systems offline for the round. The particle barbette carries a radiation effect and reaches further than anything the pack has ever brought against her.

Standing doctrine. The particle barbette is the primary kill weapon. Against a powered target the gunnery chief takes a called shot at the power plant — Gunner-6 plus Fire Control/4 less the called-shot penalty, and armour piercing from intense focus. Simulation puts a plant knockout at one to two rounds at medium range. A ship with no power is a prize; a ship blown apart is paperwork.

Fire control and the AI gunners

A Computer/25 running Fire Control/4 lays every weapon aboard. The ship's answer to a six-mount, twelve-weapon armament and a crew of five officers is that most of her guns are not crewed by people. Six AI gunnery units are installed, AG-1 through AG-6, of which four are normally crewed — one to each pop-up turret. The remaining two stand up on the barbettes when the gunnery chief and her assistant are not holding those mounts in person.

AG-3, “Gamma”, is the most articulate of the six and speaks for the others. Gamma has a documented hesitation quirk — a measurable pause before firing on a civilian silhouette, learned from a friendly-fire incident aboard a previous installation. The quirk was flagged for correction during the refit. The crew elected to keep it.

Withheld capability. At Pagaton the commander fought the entire engagement without launching fighters. Six light fighters were aboard and were never seen. As of this report the surviving elements of the Blood Profit Pack are not believed to know they exist.

Section 4 • Embarked Craft

What she carries

An auxiliary carrier on a 600-ton hull. She carries 124 tons of small craft in 96 tons of ship — and the discrepancy is not an accounting error, it is the whole trick.

A berth costs 110 per cent of the craft that sits in it, and the craft itself displaces nothing against the mother hull's budget. So six fighters at 60 tons ride in a 66-ton bay, and a 24-ton launch in a 26-ton bay. The armoured pinnace does better still: at 40 tons it is the largest thing aboard, but it rides outside, on clamps, for four tons of fittings. Forty tons of hull for the price of four — which is also why the first thing anyone sees when the Astral Dawn comes alongside is a battered pirate pinnace strapped to her back.

Wilderness refuelling, on the record. At Pagaton the ship made fuel from the atmosphere of the gas giant Hescal — forty-eight hours, nine consecutive pinnace runs, followed by two days of deceleration before contact. The Astral Dawn does not need a starport, and every operation she has run since has assumed it.

Low berths — twenty-four

Six banks of four. Sixteen berths are configured for emergency medical use — casualty stasis, holding a patient the bay cannot yet treat — and eight are standard. This is a substantial cold-storage capacity for a hull this size, and it is not decorative: it is what lets the ship take prisoners in quantity, and what lets it hold dying people until there is somewhere to put them.

Section 7 • Service History

A wreck that would not stay one

The Astral Dawn is the only vessel in District 268 service that began as a body recovery. Everything she is now was decided by people who went down to a quarantined world expecting to bring back the dead.
