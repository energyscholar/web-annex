Domain Convergence Research

Investigating cross-domain bridges between eleven scientific fields

The central claim: a small set of mathematical structures — criticality, topology, autocatalysis, and computation — keep reappearing, independently, across very different sciences. This collection maps those recurrences and tests which are genuine structural bridges rather than coincidences.

Work in progress. This is an active investigation into multicriticality and cross-domain structure. The skeleton framework, system assignments, and bridge claims are provisional hypotheses, not established results. Items marked ◆ our result are this program's own not-yet-peer-reviewed computations, distinct from textbook consensus.

This collection documents a systematic investigation into structural relationships between criticalityThe study of phase transitions — sudden qualitative changes in a system's behavior at threshold values. Power laws, scale invariance, and universality are its signatures., topologyMathematical properties preserved under continuous deformation. Braiding, knot invariants, and topological protection. Information stored in topology survives local noise., autocatalysisA network of reactions where every catalyst is produced by the network itself. Self-sustaining systems, from campfires to cells. Formalized as RAF (Reflexively Autocatalytic Food-generated) theory., computation, and seven other scientific domains. The work combines interactive tutorials with computational experiments, tracking both positive findings and null results.
