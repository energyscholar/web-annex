Major / minor theory

Major (M) properties are binary and intrinsic — a system either has topological protection or it doesn't. minor (m) properties are cumulative and extrinsic — braids compose sequentially to build computation; soliton energy accumulates along a path.

M appears when the skeleton is self-referentialThe skeleton’s properties acting on themselves. Protection protects. Self-sustenance sustains itself. These are intrinsic — they don’t need an outside.. m appears when the skeleton couples to an environment — when there is an inside and an outside.
