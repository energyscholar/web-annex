Jumps, Slowing, and the Line They Sit On

Is a jump in a growing autocatalytic chemistry on a critical line?

This is the generic method of the Criticality Compass: its Detector watches for slowing (Panel 2); its Navigator follows the line (second page).

The Question

Hypothesis (Bruce Stephenson, 2026; from informal experiments, not a result). A minimal autocatalytic set is the step at which a chemistry closes: it goes from open to closed, sometimes only barely. It is complete but not tuned — its ratios are not adjusted for speed, stability, or whatever the physical setting rewards. Adding further components tunes the ratios. Most additions change nothing; then one addition makes the system jump to a new behaviour. The hypothesis is that these jump points are points on a (multi)critical line.

A reaction set is a RAFReflexively autocatalytic and food-generated. if every reaction is catalysed by a molecule from the food setMolecules supplied from outside. or made by the set, and every reactant can be built from the food by the set’s own reactions; the largest one, the maxRAFThe unique largest RAF in a network., is found in polynomial time.1,2 In the binary polymer modelMolecules are 0/1 strings up to length n; reactions join or cut them. the catalysis level f at which RAFs appear grows linearly with maximum length n: the threshold is a line in the (n, f) plane, and the per-pair catalysis probability falls roughly exponentially in n.3,4 The onset is sharp in simulations; a zero–one law is proven for the whole network being a RAF and conjectured for any RAF existing. Whether it is first-order is not claimed.4 Kauffman proposed autocatalytic sets as a route to the origin of life;5 these pages do not argue it.

Four tests, simplest first.

  1. Make the step continuous. Ramp it in and out. Jumping at different places up and down (hysteresisUp-jump and down-jump at different control values.) marks an abrupt bifurcation. Panel 1.
  2. Watch for slowing. At a foldSaddle-node: a stable and an unstable state merge and vanish. the slowest eigenvalue heads to zero (critical slowing downRecovery from small kicks takes longer and longer.). No slowing: a structural jump. Panels 1–2.
  3. Use two controls. A line of jumps ending where the jump shrinks to zero, exponent ½ at the simplest cuspWhere two fold lines meet and the jump vanishes.. Second page, linked from the slowing case.
  4. Ensemble statistics. Heavy tails in jump sizes and quiet gaps, exponents stable across size. Panel 4.
Would confirm: hysteresis; slowing; a line of jumps ending with jump size ∝ (distance)½; size-stable heavy tails.
Would refute: a smooth crossover; a jump with no slowing; a line that never ends, or ends at a boundary without shrinking; one characteristic jump size.

Panel 1 · Ramp It, Watch It Slow

The model is the Schlögl scheme, ẋ = a x² − x³ + b − x, the classic chemical model of a first-order nonequilibrium transition with a cusp.6,11 It is a cusp by construction, so these pages demonstrate procedures, not the hypothesis. Catalyst Y raises the drive: aeff = 1.6(1 + 0.625y), ẏ = 2(c − y), b = 0.12.

Panel 2 · An Early-Warning Signal at Work

Indicators: lag-1 autocorrelation and variance on a rolling window of the detrended series,7,8 with Kendall’s τ over the stretch before the jump. Declared reading: rising if τ ≥ 0.5 for both; quiet otherwise; too short under three windows. A rising indicator is not a forecast; false alarms are common.9

The signal rose where the eigenvalue went to zero and stayed silent where it did not. Slowing is what makes a jump followable.

Panel 4 · Many Networks, Many Orders

A simplified binary polymer model, ligation only (the published one also cleaves): 0/1 strings up to length n, food = strings of length ≤ 2, each molecule catalyses each reaction with probability f/|R|. Reactions are added in random order, recomputing the maxRAF each time. An increase is a jump; additions between jumps are quiet gaps. The signature is exponents stable across n; this toy stops at n = 6, and the real test needs n ≈ 16–20 and the published generator.12
