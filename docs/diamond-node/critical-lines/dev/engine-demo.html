1 An equation trace: the two-line argument

The energy of one vortex and the entropy of where to put it both grow like ln L, so a pure number decides which wins.

Step through the terms, or drag the temperature: the free-energy line pivots through zero at kBT = πJ/2, the bare estimate in the modern convention.1 A vortexA point round which the spin angle winds by a whole turn. costs little when it is cheap to place many of them.

For the Compass

The argument is about scale (ln L); scale is what the Detector reads.

Working equations E = πJ ln(L/a),   S = 2kB ln(L/a),   F = (πJ − 2kBT) ln(L/a)

The 1973 paper prints the same argument with coefficients that differ by a convention factor.1

Full derivation

The spin-wave energy of a single vortex is (J/2)∫|∇θ|²d²r with |∇θ| = 1/r, which gives πJ∫aL dr/r = πJ ln(L/a); the vortex can sit at any of (L/a)² sites, so S = kB ln(L/a)².

2 A flow: the Kosterlitz recursion relations

Two couplings, one line of fixed points that ends: drag the start point and watch where the flow stops.

The field is dy/dℓ = −xy, dx/dℓ = −y² with x = πβ − 2, and every trajectory keeps E = y² − x² fixed (the dashed hyperbola through the start).3 On the critical branch x = y the flow approaches the end of the line only as y = 1/(1/y0 + ℓ).3

For the Compass

A flow that stops on a line reads LINE; near the line's end it slows only as 1/ℓ, and the honest verdict there is REFUSE.

Working equations dy/dt = −xy,   dx/dt = −y²,   E = y² − x²

Those are eqs 22–24 of Hasenbusch, Marcu & Pinn, with t = ln B the scale.3

4 A total table: the five-valued sign ◆ design

A velocity v with a band w, read against a declared margin ε, lands in exactly one of five cells, and “?” is one of them.

Drag v, w and ε. The cell is looked up in a table with a row for every combination of four yes/no tests; no chain of conditions decides it. The margin ε plays the role of the declared equivalence bounds of a two-one-sided-tests procedure, which must be fixed before the data are seen.4

For the Compass

With ε = 0 the table collapses to the three-valued sign (SIG+, SIG−, ?); a LINE needs ε > 0 to read ZERO!.
