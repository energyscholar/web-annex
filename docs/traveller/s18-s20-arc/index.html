⭐ The through-line, and it is not the pirates

The maiden voyage made them famous, and fame is incompatible with a Q-ship. (R-28) Every session in this arc is a consequence of that: at Bowman they are recognised, at Flammarion they are re-tooled, and at Raschev their notoriety becomes the only instrument that can solve the problem.

They spent eight months becoming exactly the wrong people for their job, and exactly the right people for the next one.

Branch map — S18's three outcomes

All three paths reach Flammarion. This is deliberate. The arc must not depend on a 30% roll.
  • NOT FOUND (70%) — the pirates stay a live thread into S21+. S18 loses nothing, because its real content is the crew crisis.
  • STRIKE (15%) — ⚠ the hardest to run. See the combat note in S18.
  • MARK & LEAVE (15%) — ⭐ the best outcome for the arc. They carry coordinates to a naval base. It makes S19 bigger, and it hands the Navy a problem the crew can't solve alone.

S18 · Bowman — "Nobody Comes Out Here"

What the session is actually about

Not the pirates. The end of the crew. Bowman is where they discover that attentive belters recognise the Heroes of Raschev from the Collace news, that the manifest carrying no names doesn't help, and that a Q-ship crewed by celebrities is not a Q-ship (R-28).

Beats

  1. Emergence & the mail. ~8 months of accumulated correspondence in one lump — all of it 3–5 months stale. The Raschev letters were written before Nyrthus happened. ⭐ They cannot answer home by mail; the round trip is longer than any of the problems will wait.
  2. The filings. Arabus and Nyrthus reports, sanitised (R-01.2 — no psi in the logs). Asao's unsoftened Amber/Red assessment lands in front of the Scouts.
  3. Recognition. Someone knows who they are. Not hostile — interested. This is the session's turn.
  4. The hunt (optional, player-driven) — see below.
  5. The decision. Who leaves. Where next. What the ship becomes.

⛔ The hunt: official channels are a designed dead end (R-35)

The IISS base and the corporations will give them nothing. Not obstruction — irrelevance. Officialdom doesn't go where there's no money, and the Deep Claim Consortium's respectable face is aimed precisely at the Scouts and at Halden Reach Minerals.

⇒ The paydirt is with the poorest haulers and the people on the margins — independent tug skippers, volatiles harvesters working Belt III because they can't afford to buy fuel, a Red Ink clerk, a belter medic.

⚠ The crew's correct professional instinct is the wrong instinct. Being right about procedure is what costs them the trail.

The three things that actually crack it

  • Max's TL-15 medbay. TL-9 mining with no law maims people, and Halden Reach's good clinic is for Halden Reach people only. Treating an independent's crush injury is a debt that can't be refused or explained away.
  • Being the Heroes of Raschev. The thing that ruins them as spies is what makes them worth a belter's risk.
  • ⭐ The consumption anomaly (R-30). There is no pirate transponder in any log — they're careful. The tell is the DCC's own legitimate fuel account, running far above what its known operations could burn. Nothing is forged; the numbers are just too big. It requires a baseline to mean anything ⇒ two facts, two sources, combined. Investigation, not perception. No roll surfaces it — a question does.

⚠ If they strike (15%) — read this first

They have never faced Howl of Profit — Oeg's 400 t Type-P flagship, 3 triple turrets, sandcaster, 50 t pinnace, J-2/4G. Plus a 400 t converted merchant, a 100 t scout, smallcraft, the 1,000 t J-6 tug, and 2–3 prizes possibly armed from cached scrap using the base's own fabrication shops.

⭐ But the pirates have studied the tape — they hold sensor records of Pagaton and know the AD's sandcasters and ion cannons. Do not replay Pagaton and expect Pagaton's result.

⭐⭐ And they still do not know about the six Tlatl fighters (R-24/R-38). At Pagaton Delleron held them until the scout had jumped, then released them. The witness left first. This is the banked payoff — spend it here only if the session earns it.

⛔ The base is a 1,000 t colony ship full of people, including the consortium's civilians. A clean kill is not available. That is the point.

2. The crew change ⭐ and the new ship

This is where R-28 comes due. Some of them are burned as covert operators. The AD stays, they don't — or the reverse. ⛳ Bruce: the PCs leave the Astral Dawn behind and take some other hull, TBD.

Candidate hulls — Flammarion is an A-class yard with naval backing, so almost anything is arguable:
  • The 200 t Far Trader prize from Walston, if taken (R-25) — small enough that JSI didn't claim it. Their own ship, earned.
  • Blood Fang, the 400 t corsair, out of refit at Kethay — ⚠ but she's JSI's and famous.
  • A purpose-built hull from the yard — JSI rewarding the intelligence coup. Fast, clean, and it makes them owe the Navy.
  • ⭐ Something unglamorous and anonymous — the thematic answer. They are famous now; the ship should not be.

3. Vince's plea arrives

Via Marta Aliyev — Vince's niece (R-02), the compound's ORACLE correspondent. ⚠ 3–5 months one way. Whatever it describes is already worse.

The ask, in Vince's words: bring warships. ⭐ And remember what Vince is owed: Garoo leaned on him to give the crew up, and he refused (R-31). This is not a favour requested. It is a debt being called by a man who already paid one.

The Garoo factions — ⭐ the complication that makes S20 work

Horaz Sutyn has been overthrown. He killed Ryoc IV, took power as a reformist dictator, ordered the Health Police stood down — and they carried on regardless. Then the hypocrisy crisis destroyed what his order couldn't, the street finished the job, and it took him with it. Garoo is now four factions and a provisional council that governs none of them.

⛔⛔ THE KNOT — three things, three holders, and none of them will speak to each other

The faction with the warships over Raschev is not the faction that can pay Raschev, and neither is the faction that can lawfully sign anything. That is why this cannot be solved from inside Garoo.

⭐⭐ Why any treaty needs all three

SIC must stand the guns down. The Yards must build and staff. The Sutyn remnant must sign — because a treaty the Imperium will not recognise is not a treaty, and the council's signature is worth nothing offworld.

Guns, capability, legitimacy. Three factions, three holdings, and a blood feud between each pair.

⭐⭐⭐ THE CREW'S PROBLEM: their friends are the ones pointing the guns

The offworld-agitator theory is COMPLETELY TRUE. The crew did help bring down the Health Police. They left a large footprint on Garoo and established multiple contacts inside SIC. Garoovians in the know are aware the Heroes of Raschev were there and were involved.

⇒ So the crew are not strangers arriving to mediate. They are veterans of one side. SIC won the revolution the crew helped start — and SIC is now holding a bombardment threat over the crew's own home world, because 600 million people are hungry and their money is worthless.

Nobody here is a villain. The crew's allies are the aggressors, for a reason the crew helped create.

⭐ The asset already in place

Thomas — SIC member, refugee, living at the crew's island compound on Raschev, witness to Asao's innocence, and holding encrypted resistance comms. ⇒ A live, secure channel into SIC, sitting on their own island, put there by their own past kindness. Bob is with him. Kella leads SIC on Garoo.

⛔ And the thing that protects them is a lie

The published investigative account debunks the agitator theory — on the grounds that the only evidence was a fabricated recording. The debunking is correct about the evidence and wrong about the conclusion. ⇒ The crew are shielded by a forgery, and any SIC contact could confirm the truth in a sentence.

⛔ Max must never be traced. SIC would build him a statue. The Yards would execute him.

Why only these five people can broker it

  • SIC trusts them because it is true. Not reputation — shared history, and named contacts.
  • ⭐ Asao's warrant came from the Health Police, which makes him a martyr to the faction holding the guns.
  • The Yards need an outsider with JSI standing and no stake in the blood feud — the only guarantor either side would accept.
  • Raschev will listen because they are landholders, local heroes, and friends of the head of state.
  • ⚠ But they are not neutral, and both sides will eventually work that out.

S20 · The Raschev Crisis — "Payment Due"

The situation, stated plainly

Garoo warships hold station over Raschev, threatening bombardment and demanding the food ships be filled as usual. Raschev has no defence whatsoever — TL-6, starport C, no navy.

⭐ It is a payments crisis, not a war

Garoo's currency died with its government. There is no reliable replacement that works on Raschev. Garoo cannot pay — so it has reached for the only lever a desperate polity has left. Nobody involved is a villain. The Authority is trying to feed 600 million people.

⇒ The military solution loses. The monetary one wins. That is exactly why it is beyond Vince, and exactly what "shuttle diplomacy" means here.

⭐⭐⭐ The resolution: Garoo pays in capability

An A-class shipyard, TL-10 industry, and an intact unstaffed medical service. Raschev has 20 million people, TL-6, no doctors worth the name, and a system-defence gap Eric has been raising since before the crew ever left.

The hostage-takers become the shipwrights. Garoo builds Raschev's system defence and staffs its hospitals, and eats.

⛔ But the Authority holds the guns and the Yards hold the shipyard. The deal requires the revolution's winners to hand their only leverage to the people who used to arrest them — and requires the Yards to arm a world at the request of a faction that wants their protectees hanged. That is the negotiation. That is the session.

Shape of play

  1. Arrival & the standoff. One capable hull against ~12 converted merchants with no capital ships. ⭐ Deterrence works; shooting is a catastrophe — these are desperate men, not pirates.
  2. Raschev side. Vince, Marta, Eric & Signe (siblings, R-03), the compound, the island. Asao's estate — which he has visited exactly once.
  3. Garoo side — ground operations. ⭐ Bruce: dangerous ground ops that let the PCs flex the full roster. Arcology transit, the Summersun-run port, a Yards facility, a Directorate-remnant armoury. Asao's marines finally matter again.
  4. Shuttle diplomacy. Neither faction will meet the other. Someone must physically carry terms, and be trusted by both.
  5. The signing — or the failure. ⚠ Failure is a real outcome: the food ships fill at gunpoint, Raschev is looted, and the crew learn what their fame is not worth.
