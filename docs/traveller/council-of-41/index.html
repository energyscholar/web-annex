The Council of 41: who is bent

You asked who on the Council is corrupt. The honest answer is that some of them are, I can prove a little of it, and I suspect a good deal more. The forty-one are the Provisional Authority's governing body. They sit in Arco-1, they alone can pass an amnesty, and they do not vote as forty-one. They vote as seven blocs. Two of those blocs mean every word of the revolution. Three are filling their pockets or their files. One will vote for whoever controls the paperwork, and that is its leader. Read the tags. I have tried not to dress up a rumour as a fact. I spent twenty years catching other men doing that.

You will find the names blacked out. That is not distrust. It is how they stay alive, and how you stay useful to me. The redactions are mine, made before this left my hands, and there is no other copy. You get what was learned and how sure I am of it. You do not get who told me.
