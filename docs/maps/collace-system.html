Environment

Ocean world with scattered island landmasses. The very thin atmosphere (0.1–0.4 atm at surface) and 80% ocean coverage drove Collace's civilization underwater. Most arcology clusters are submerged 6–9 meters below the ocean surface, where the water column adds the missing 0.6–0.9 atm to bring internal air pressure to a comfortable ~1 atm. The structures use the diving-bell principle — open at the bottom, no airlocks required. Cargo and submarine traffic enters from below; sunlight filters through translucent dome ceilings above. Older island-based arcologies still exist but require sealed pressure domes — costlier and less desirable.

All outdoor activity (surface or above-water) requires filter respirator combination mask. The thin atmosphere provides little shielding from Gorie's radiation — sunburn protection mandatory. Respirator design is a fashion industry. Going maskless signals either poverty or bravado.

Gravity is 0.7G. Low-G adapted crew may experience slight disorientation in the first hours. Collaceans trend taller than Imperial standard.

Crime Advisory

Collace is a high-crime planet including violent crimes involving firearms. Offworlders are preferred targets. Exercise standard caution in lower arcology levels and at downport.

Starport & Orbital

Collace Highport is a wheel station with five docking arms. Heavy traffic — typically 30-50 vessels in port. Full shipyard facilities (annual maintenance, repair, minor modifications). Refined fuel at standard rates.

Ground downport adjacent to Port Kessler arcology cluster. Landing pads under filter domes. Commercial district: hotels, brokerage offices, shipping agents.

Fuel alternatives: Gas giant Enborne permits standard fuel skimming.

System-wide IISS presence: Scout facilities extend beyond Kirkton to half a dozen other planets and satellites. Collace's armed forces provide ground and close-orbit defense for both Collace and IISS facilities.

Commerce & Industry

Primary industries: advanced manufacturing, pharmaceutical research, electronics fabrication, financial services. Collace is District 268's economic center. Standard trade goods command premium prices; high-tech goods are locally produced and competitively priced.

Major corporate presences: SuSAG (pharmaceuticals), Tukera Lines (trade), McClellan Factors (homegrown brokerage), Oberlindes Lines.

Aslan Presence

Significant. Established community of approximately 50,000 in the Aslan Quarter (Arco-3, floors 40-60). Hierate trade mission (Arco Prime, diplomatic row), clan commercial establishments, combat gymnasium. Trade representative can relay messages to Hierate-border houses.

Fuel Options

Highport: Refined fuel at standard rates. No queue expected.

Enborne: Gas giant fuel skimming. Standard procedures. ~5.2 AU from primary (several hours transit).

Wilderness: Collace's oceans permit wilderness refueling if needed.

Enborne Moon System

Four populated moons orbiting the gas giant Enborne. A mini-civilization with combined population over 100,000. Cathay is the largest settlement (F-class port, TL-13). Alcalall's exotic fluid ocean is of scientific interest. All moons accessible via standard in-system transit.

Political Landscape

Collace has been debating Imperial membership for over twenty years. Two major factions:

Pro-Imperial Faction

Advocates naval protection, trade access, rule of law. Support base: upper-arcology professionals, military families, trade companies seeking Imperial market access.

Anti-Imperial Faction

Argues self-sufficiency: "We built TL-13 without Imperial help." Support base: industrial workers, nationalists, Trexalon sympathizers. Some legitimate political movement, some external influence of undetermined origin.

Trexalon

Anti-Imperial technocracy two parsecs trailing (1339). Economic rival, mutual espionage, "cold war" posture. Comparable TL and population. The Trexalon Technical Consortium (TTC) opposes Imperial membership for D268 — perceives the Imperium as impediment to its expansionist ambitions. Historical friction predates current membership debate.

Key flashpoint: Pavabid (1238) — iridium deposits. Both sides competing for influence with the xenophobic Church of Stellar Divinity theocracy.

Assessment: Collace political environment is complex and factional. Crew should avoid public political statements.

History

Originally a Sindalian Empire mining colony. Population was in serious decline when found by Darrian scouts in -1310, who provided technological advice enabling self-sustainable infrastructure. Over two millennia of steady growth to ~1 billion. Settlers from Collace colonized Inchin and Avastan, though Collace has effectively disowned their governments. Colmetal, Inc. (registered on Collace) maintains mines throughout the subsector.

Hazards & Advisories

Atmospheric Taint MANDATORY

Filter respirator required outside sealed environments. Taint concentration spikes during weather events — monitor local atmospheric alerts. Suit integrity checks recommended every 8 hours for surface EVA. Sunburn risk is high — thin atmosphere provides minimal UV shielding from Gorie.

High Crime ADVISORY

Violent crime including firearms. Offworlders are preferred targets. Lower arcology levels and downport commercial areas are highest risk. Exercise standard caution.

Political Surveillance AWARENESS

Government 4 + active political factions = surveillance infrastructure. Communications may be monitored. Standard OPSEC protocols apply.

Trexalon Espionage AWARENESS

Collace counter-intelligence is active. Foreign nationals may attract attention. Maintain cover documentation.

Arcology Navigation

Most arcology clusters are submerged 6–9 meters below the ocean surface (diving-bell principle — open at the bottom, 1 atm internal pressure from water column). Entry from above by shuttle to surface pads, then elevator down; entry from below by submarine or boat through the open base. No respirator needed inside underwater arcologies. Older island-based arcologies use sealed pressure domes — respirator protocols apply in transition zones. Lower-level areas are poorly mapped and functionally lawless despite Law Level 3 nominal designation.
