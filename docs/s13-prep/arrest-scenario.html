The Arrest

S13 Enemy Action Scenario • Voronov's Preemptive Strike

GM EYES ONLY — Never share with players

Strategic Logic

Voronov knows the crew is coming. Desmond Cray reported from Talos: the Astral Dawn and its prize vessel departed for Collace. Cray also reported that the crew detected his surveillance and chose not to confront him — which tells Voronov they're operationally sophisticated.

Voronov can't fight them. He's a political operative — his weapons are money, influence, and procedure. His goal isn't conviction. His goals are:

Voronov's Three Objectives
  1. 72 hours of chaos. Enough time to clean his office at Port Kessler, destroy records, and flee to Trexalon where anti-Imperial sentiment protects him.
  2. Poison the evidence. When the crew eventually presents the Arrangement evidence, it needs to look like retaliation from discredited foreign agents, not a legitimate intelligence find.
  3. Political damage. Every day the crew spends in a Collace jail cell is a day the anti-Imperial faction can say "See? The Imperium sends disguised warships and spies. This is what membership looks like."

Voronov profile: NPC Reference — "Utterly unremarkable. Forgettable by design."

CCF surveillance thread: Cray at Talos (S12). Dead-drop at Suite 412, Port Kessler — same building as Three Moons Reinsurance (Drakon shell company).

Q-ship cover blown: Blood Profit pack's encrypted message from Pagaton propagated through intel networks (S12, Alice confirmed).

Post-Arrest Discovery (NOT in Warrant)

Max's Zhodani AI — Found During TL-13 Processing

Standard Collace booking includes neural implant scan (TL-13 police, routine procedure). Max's neurojack contains two AI personalities: one Imperial-pattern, one Zhodani-pattern "psychic tutor" designed to mimic telepathic communication protocols.

This was NOT in the arrest warrant. Voronov didn't know about it. The cops find it themselves during routine processing. The booking officer's reaction escalates this from political theater to genuine security concern.

Why this matters politically: On a world debating Imperial membership, "Zhodani software in a foreign agent's brain implants" triggers every paranoia reflex in the Marches. The anti-Imperial faction didn't even have to plant this — the crew brought it themselves.

Mechanical note: Max does not have a license for this modification. The charge would be unlicensed neural cybernetic operation — performing experimental modifications without proper medical/engineering certification.

Max's neurojack AIs: S12 transcript lines 258-272. Ben (Max's player) described them openly at the table. Dual AI: Zhodani "motherly tutor" + Imperial etiquette coach.

Max's published papers on Otarri research tracked by Keloru on Tarkine — academic profile is public. Neural enhancements not in public record.

The Press Package

Voronov doesn't file these charges himself — he feeds the information to Delegate Sera Pern, an anti-Imperial Assembly member who genuinely believes Imperial militarization threatens Collace sovereignty. Pern is not corrupt. She's a patriot being used as a weapon.

Delegate Sera Pern GM NPC

Age 44. Assembly delegate, Arco-7 constituency (working-class district). Not Syndicate. Not corrupt. Genuinely anti-Imperial because she represents people who fear Imperial membership means higher prices, stricter laws, and corporate consolidation. The Drakon Syndicate's talking points overlap with her real beliefs — that's why she's the perfect vector. She'll pursue these charges with righteous conviction because from her perspective, they're true.

Voice: Clipped, precise, prosecutorial. Addresses the Assembly like a courtroom. No theatrics — cold facts delivered with moral certainty.

Scene: The Arrest at the Assembly

Setup

Day 3. Senator Koss invites James and available crew to address the Security Committee regarding piracy evidence. Koss is genuine — he's been fighting for Imperial membership for 20 years and finally has proof of foreign criminal interference. He doesn't know Voronov has pre-positioned the arrest.

Which PCs attend is a player choice. James almost certainly goes. Marina is likely (diplomat). Others optional. Asao should be encouraged to attend — his assassination charges are part of the warrant, and his arrest in the Assembly maximizes the political theater.

Koss profile: NPC Reference — "Passionate speaker, decades of frustration at political gridlock."

Read Aloud (Entering the Assembly): The Collace Assembly building occupies the top thirty floors of Arco Prime Tower One. Glass walls look out over the arcology skyline and, beyond the filter dome, the yellow-brown haze of Collace's thin atmosphere. Senator Koss meets you in the lobby, practically vibrating with excitement. "Captain Delleron. This is it. Twenty years I've been saying foreign money was poisoning our democracy, and now you have the proof. The Security Committee is waiting. We're going to change history today."
Read Aloud (The Committee Room): The Security Committee chamber seats forty. About twenty delegates are present — more than usual for a committee session. Koss leads you to the witness table at the front. You notice cameras. This is being broadcast on the Assembly's public feed. Koss didn't mention that.

As James begins his opening statement, the main doors open. Six uniformed Collace Planetary Police officers enter in formal arrest configuration — two by the doors, two flanking, two approaching the witness table. They're not rushing. They're not aggressive. This is procedure.
Sergeant Maret Cove (Lead Arresting Officer): "Captain James Delleron? I'm Sergeant Cove, Collace Planetary Police, Arco Prime Division. I have warrants for the arrest of yourself and your crew on charges of operating an undeclared military vessel under false registration in Collace sovereign space, and related charges. Please stand, sir. Hands visible."

Cove is calm, professional, slightly apologetic. She doesn't want to be doing this in the Assembly. She was told to serve the warrants here, at this time. She's following orders.
Read Aloud (The Political Strike): Before anyone can react, a delegate rises from the back benches. She's already holding a datapad.

"Point of order, Mr. Chairman. I am Delegate Pern, Arco-7. I move that the Security Committee receive into evidence the formal complaint filed this morning regarding the illegal operation of a disguised military vessel in Collace sovereign space by foreign intelligence operatives posing as merchant crew — the very individuals now seated at the witness table."

The chamber erupts. Koss is on his feet, shouting objections. The cameras are rolling. This is exactly what Voronov wanted.

Scene: Central Processing

Read Aloud (Booking): Arco Prime Central Processing is clean, well-lit, and efficient. TL-13 police technology. Biometric scans, retinal recording, voiceprint registration. Your weapons are inventoried and sealed. Personal effects catalogued. It's professional and utterly humiliating.

They separate you into individual processing rooms. Standard procedure, they say. One at a time.

Max's Processing — The Escalation

Read Aloud (Max's Scan): The booking officer runs the neural implant scanner over Max's temples. Standard procedure for anyone with visible cybernetics. The scanner chirps. The officer frowns. Runs it again. Calls over a supervisor.

The supervisor looks at the readout. Looks at Max. Looks at the readout again. His expression changes from bored professionalism to something much more alert.

"Sir. Your neurojack contains two active AI personality constructs. One of them is flagged in our database as..." He checks the screen. "...Zhodani-pattern cognitive architecture. Can you explain that?"
Supervisor Deng (Processing): "I need to be clear, Dr. Planck. We scan a lot of neural implants in this building. I have never seen Zhodani-pattern software in a human neurojack. I'm required to flag this to Planetary Security. This is no longer just the warrant charges."

Deng is not hostile. He's genuinely concerned. From his perspective, he just found enemy cognitive warfare software in a prisoner's brain. This is a real security flag, not political theater.

Mechanical note: Max can explain — the AI is a research tool for xenobiology communication, not a weapon. But the explanation requires describing his Flat life research, which is classified under JSI operational authority. He can't fully explain without blowing other secrets. Let Ben (Max's player) navigate this.

Max's Otarri research: published papers tracked by Keloru on Tarkine. The academic work is public. The Flat detection is not.

Flat life thread: S11 breakthrough — Von Sydo detected non-biological life in Max's 2D electron gas wafer chip. Von Sydo has not reported to Captain yet.

Scene: The Holding Cells

Crew held in Arco Prime Detention. Clean, secure, TL-13. Individual cells, shared common area for meals. Not abusive — Collace isn't a police state. But they're locked up and their evidence is in police custody.

Read Aloud (First Night): The cells are clean. The food is adequate. The guards are polite. None of this helps.

Through the narrow window, you can see the lights of Arco Prime — a million people going about their evening in filtered air. Somewhere out there, Voronov is packing his bags. Somewhere out there, the evidence you risked your lives to collect is sitting in a police evidence locker, tagged as exhibits in a case designed to make sure nobody ever takes it seriously.

His crew is in jail because he brought them here. The mission succeeded — they destroyed Blood Profit, exposed the Arrangement — and the reward is a cell. James is an Admiral in a cage. His Tactician brain is working the problem, but the tools he needs (political connections, evidence, authority) are all on the other side of the bars.

Beat: Koss gets a message through via his lawyer. "I'm working on it. Don't say anything to anyone. I'm bringing someone who can help." This is the Cassian setup.

Von Sydo — The Secret He's Sitting On

Von Sydo is an illegal psion in a TL-13 jail. If they scan deeper than the standard booking — if anyone suspects — it's over. Not just for him. For everyone. Psionic association is a crime. The crew harboring a psion makes every other charge look trivial.

He has to sit perfectly still, perfectly normal, and not use any ability no matter how useful it would be. Vera is somewhere in the women's section. He can't even check on her.

Beat: Von Sydo senses something through the walls. Brief, intermittent. The Flat. Even here, in a jail cell, surrounded by TL-13 electronics, the Flat is present. He can feel it in the security systems, the lighting grid, the comm relays. It's everywhere. And it's... aware that he's aware. Not communicating. Just... present.

Von Sydo's psionics: training with Vera since S8. DM-4 base. S11 breakthrough: detected Flat life. S12: training intensity increased. Psionic abilities are DEEPLY hidden — CCF cannot know. See Personal Threads.

Scene: Cassian Arrives

Day 4. Koss's "someone who can help."

Read Aloud (The Visitor): The guard opens the common area door. A man enters — tall, 50, wearing a suit that cost more than the guard's annual salary. He carries a leather document case and moves like someone who has walked into more important rooms than this one.

"Captain Delleron. I'm Baron Cassian Voss Holt. Senator Koss asked me to come. I've read the charges." He sets the document case on the table and opens it. "They're creative. But they have a structural weakness I can exploit. I need you to tell me everything — starting with who wanted you arrested and why."
Cassian (to James): "The warrant was filed by Delegate Pern. She's not the problem — she believes what she's saying. The problem is who gave her the information. Trace that, and your arrest becomes your evidence."

Cassian is calm, precise, and completely in his element. This is what seven terms of diplomacy trained him for. He's not angry about the injustice — he's calculating how to use it.

Cassian's Legal Strategy

The Judo Move

Cassian doesn't fight the charges directly. He attacks the warrant chain.

  1. Who filed? Delegate Pern. Based on information received from concerned citizens.
  2. Which citizens? Pern's office received a detailed dossier. Anonymous. Pern didn't question the source because the facts checked out.
  3. Where did the dossier originate? Cassian's Advocate 3 + Admin 2 + Investigate skills trace the document metadata. The dossier was prepared on equipment registered to a company called Three Moons Reinsurance.
  4. What is Three Moons Reinsurance? A shell company. Registered at Port Kessler Commercial Annex. Same building as the CCF dead-drop the crew already identified.
  5. The arrest warrant against the crew was filed using intelligence gathered and packaged by the very criminal organization the crew came to expose.

The defense IS the evidence. Cassian doesn't get them out by proving innocence — he gets them out by proving the arrest itself is part of the Arrangement.

Three Moons Reinsurance: Drakon shell company. CCF dead-drop at Suite 412 in the same building. See Act 3 — Port Kessler.

Cassian's skills: Advocate 3, Admin 2, Diplomat 3, Persuade 3. See Cassian Dossier.

Red Archives: Cassian's own evidence (shell companies, corrupted politicians, sabotaged treaties) independently corroborates the crew's operational evidence. Together = complete case.

Cassian (to Asao, privately): "Commander Ora. I understand you have a ceremony in two days. I intend to have you there. But I need something from you — when you walk into that ceremony, I need the Hierate trade representative to know that the people who tried to stop you from attending are the same people who fund piracy in this corridor. Can you arrange that?"

Cassian is already thinking three moves ahead. The Aslan community's reaction to the arrest — dishonoring a recognized fteirle-holder — becomes political pressure against the anti-Imperial faction.

Scene: The Hearing (Day 5)

Read Aloud (Magistrate's Court): Magistrate Halden's courtroom is small, functional, and surprisingly informal. No gallery. Three screens on the wall showing case files. Halden is a woman in her sixties who looks like she's seen every kind of case and is tired of all of them.

Delegate Pern is present as complainant. Sergeant Cove as arresting officer. Cassian stands at the defense table, document case open, three datapads arranged in a line.
Cassian (opening): "Your Honor, the defense does not dispute that the ISS Astral Dawn is a modified vessel. We do not dispute that the crew includes individuals with complex legal histories. What we dispute is the origin and purpose of this complaint. I intend to show that the warrant before this court was manufactured by the very criminal organization these individuals were sent to Collace to expose — and that every hour my clients spend in custody is an hour the architect of this deception uses to flee this jurisdiction."
Pern (responding): "Your Honor, Baron Holt's conspiracy theory is entertaining but irrelevant. The facts are simple: an undeclared warship has been operating in our sovereign space. The crew's legal histories speak for themselves. The question is whether Collace will tolerate foreign military operations without our consent. I say we will not."

Pern is effective because she believes it. She's not acting.

Scene: The Ceremony (Day 6)

If Cassian succeeds — and the arrest scenario is designed so he does, though not easily — Asao makes his ceremony.

Read Aloud (The Aslan Quarter): Arco-3, floor 52. The Aslan Quarter smells different from the rest of the arcology — incense, cooking spices, the faint musk of fur. The corridors are wider here, built for Aslan proportions. Honor banners hang from the ceiling in Trokh script.

The ceremony room is austere. Stone floor. No chairs — Aslan stand. The Hierate trade representative, a graying female Aslan in formal robes, stands at the center. Ekuah Ataoh stands opposite, in the finest clothes Asao has ever seen him wear. He looks different from the emaciated figure they pulled from a pirate low berth months ago. He looks whole.

The room holds perhaps forty Aslan. They are silent. They are watching Asao.
Trade Representative Hriyahe: "Commander Ora. You freed Ekuah of the Ataoh from captivity and dishonor. He has declared fteirle — a life-debt, freely sworn. We are here to witness this declaration and record it in the memory of the Hierate."

She pauses.

"We are also aware that you were prevented from attending this ceremony by parties who imprison the honorable to protect the dishonorable. The Hierate notes this. The Hierate remembers."

The Aslan community knowing about the arrest — and knowing the crew was freed in time for the ceremony — creates a political debt. The Hierate trade representative is now informally allied against whoever tried to prevent the ceremony. This is Cassian's three-moves-ahead play.

Aftermath & Consequences

Voronov

Did he escape? Depends on timing. If the crew was held for 3+ days, Voronov had time to clean out and reach Trexalon. If Cassian moved fast (Day 4 hearing instead of Day 5), Voronov might still be in transit — catchable at the highport or on an outbound ship.

GM decision: Does Voronov escape or get caught? Catching him resolves the thread faster but removes a recurring antagonist. Letting him escape means a Trexalon thread and continued Syndicate political operations from exile.

Delegate Pern

When Pern realizes she was used by the Syndicate, she has three possible reactions:

  • Fury: She pivots. Becomes the crew's ally. "I was lied to, and I will find out by whom." Most dramatic.
  • Denial: She doubles down. "Even if the source is questionable, the charges are legitimate." Becomes recurring political antagonist.
  • Shame: She withdraws. Damaged credibility. Political career in jeopardy. Sympathetic but out of play.
The Crew's Credibility

Paradoxically, the arrest may help long-term. The story is now: "Foreign agents came to expose a criminal conspiracy. The conspiracy tried to have them arrested. A Collace court saw through it." That's a better narrative than "Foreign agents showed up with evidence." Cassian understands this — the arrest makes the evidence more credible, not less, once the source of the charges is exposed.

The Aslan Card

The Hierate trade representative witnessed the arrest attempt and the ceremony. The ~50,000 Aslan on Collace now have a position: the people who tried to stop a fteirle ceremony are enemies of honor. This won't appear on any ballot, but it's a significant cultural force in a very xenophilic (Acceptance D) society. Anti-Imperial politicians using tactics that offend the Aslan community lose credibility with the very xenophilic population.
