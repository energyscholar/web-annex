The Arcology Society

~1 billion people in arcology clusters. GM EMBELLISHMENT Collace's very thin atmosphere (~0.1–0.4 atm surface pressure) and 80% ocean coverage make underwater arcologies the dominant settlement pattern. Submerged 6–9 meters below the surface, the water column provides the missing 0.6–0.9 atm — bringing internal pressure to a comfortable ~1 atm. The structures are open at the bottom (diving-bell principle): air stays in, water stays out, no airlocks needed for submarine cargo traffic. Cities hang from the underside of the sea surface, lit by sunlight filtering through translucent dome ceilings. Island-based arcologies exist but are older, requiring sealed pressure domes — more expensive and less desirable. The best real estate is underwater.

Canon: middle/upper classes gravitate to newer high-security arcos, leaving older structures to working class and poor. GM EMBELLISHMENT This maps naturally: upper arcos are the newest deep-water structures with best light and pressure regulation; lower arcos and undercity are the older island-based sealed domes and sub-basements where maintenance has lapsed and pressure seals leak.

Act 2 — Kirkton Debrief (30 min)

The Mandatory Beat

This is the session's dramatic core. Commander Vasquez has been tracking their progress through coded scout courier messages. The crew has exceeded expectations — destroying Blood Profit was the mission; uncovering the Collace Arrangement is a bonus she didn't anticipate.

Commander Lira Vasquez — JSI D268 Station Chief

Age 52. Calm, speaks softly, expects results. She planned the Q-ship operation and selected the crew. Bureaucratically careful — wants to verify every piece of evidence before acting.

Knows: Crew's mission success (Blood Profit), general corridor intel.

Wants: Full intelligence dump. Every piece of evidence catalogued.

Doesn't know: The Arrangement. Nuclear weapon intel. Full scope of Drakon operations. Von Sydo's psionics.

Vasquez (opening): "Captain Delleron. Welcome to Kirkton. I've been reading your message drops for months. Let's start with the Blood Profit Pack and work forward. Tell me everything."

Debrief Flow

  1. Blood Profit report — Vasquez expects this. Standard debrief. Hazard pay authorized.
  2. Q-ship cover blown — Vasquez needs to know. Changes operational posture. "That complicates things considerably."
  3. Nuclear weapon intel — This escalates to Priority Alpha. Vasquez gets visibly concerned. "That changes the threat assessment for the entire corridor."
  4. The Arrangement — When the crew reveals what they found in the pirate data. Vasquez pauses. "You're telling me the piracy is a symptom, not the disease." She asks: "How do you want to handle this?"
