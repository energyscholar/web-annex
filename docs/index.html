Interactive tutorials on the edges where systems change, and what it takes to tell where one is

Ticket hall

Edges are where behaviour changes: water boils, a magnet loses its pull, a dry forest suddenly burns. Near an edge a small push has a large effect, and unrelated systems start to behave alike. Some systems, people say, are poised: they seem to sit on their edge rather than fall off it. This site is one line of stations that shows what an edge is, how an edge can be a whole line, how you would tell, and what instrument it would take to check.
