The Criticality Compass

A designed instrument for finding and following multivariate criticality

Status

This page restates a design. Nothing in it has been shown to work. The design is fixed by a deposited design note (version 0.1, 2026);1 results, each with its status, belong in the methods paper that the note says will follow.

1What it is

Multivariate criticality (MC) is critical behaviour spread across more than one tuning parameter: a line of critical points or of fixed pointsAt a fixed point one renormalisation step leaves the observable unchanged (see the needle, Section 2)., or a point where such lines meet or end. The Criticality Compass is a design for an instrument that finds MC in a system, follows its lines, and eventually maps their topology.1 Its targets include the BKTBerezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless. line, lines of continuously varying exponents, and tricritical points.

The design has two parts. The Detector takes a declared input, reads each declared point and returns one verdict word; a family layer then reads a run of points and returns one family word. Every word falls under one of three answers: MC, Not MC, or Can’t tell (Table 1). On Not MC the Detector reports the side class it saw and stops. On Can’t tell it refuses, and the reason is a value of the output. On MC it hands the declared plane and the MC points to the Navigator, which is designed to run in a loop with the simulation or experiment that produces the data: it receives each reading, places it on one side of the line or on it, and returns the next setting. Each trace stops at a typed terminal, and traced lines with their end and meeting points are meant to accumulate into a map of the MC topology. In the current code the two Navigator followers are run directly; the Detector-gated hand-off is the design.1

2What is measured

The measurement uses a Monte Carlo block-spin renormalisation-group comparison,10,11 finite-size scaling9 and a replicate interval. Every quantity the decision reads is a difference of a declared observable across a declared relation, computed by a small routine that takes no decisions. The relations are the edges on which differences are taken: size pairs, matched blocking levels, a chain of control points, and a pair of startsThe declared starts of the chains. The design compares an ordered start with a random one, and a start must be shown equilibrated..1

The central instrument is the needle. Let g1(L, p) be the declared observable of a system of linear size L after p applications of a blocking map of factor b. The needle compares size L at level p+1 with size L/b at level p; both then have the same block-lattice sizeL / bp+1: the lattice size left after p+1 blocking steps., one blocking step apart. The flow velocity is

v = g1(L, p+1) − g1(L/b, p).

If v is zero within its margin, one renormalisation step leaves the observable unchanged at that size: a fixed point at that size. Its sign gives the direction of flow, toward order or disorder. Near a trivial sink v is also near zero, for a trivial reason, so the needle also reads the differences from the declared ordered and disordered sink values.1

The interval

Each statistic is formed within each of m independent chains, with chain k of one cell paired by index with chain k of every other. The estimate is the median of the per-chain values t1…tm, and the interval is [t(r), t(m+1−r)] in the order statistics. For independent draws from any continuous distribution it contains the median of the per-chain statistic with probability at least

1 − 2 P( Bin(m, ½) ≤ r − 1 ),

the classical rank interval for a median.12 The bound is distribution-free and needs no autocorrelation time.1

The sign cell

Every coded quantity is an interval [xlo, xhi] for a change, with a declared margin ε ≥ 0. The cuts are the sorted points (−ε, 0, +ε). Let nlo count the cuts strictly below xlo and nhi the cuts at or below xhi. The ten reachable pairs are looked up in a total table that yields eight values: SIG-, NEG, SLOW-, ZERO!, SLOW+, POS, SIG+ and UNRES. There is no comparison chain and no fall-through. With ε = 0 only SIG-, UNRES and SIG+ are reachable: the three-valued sign. A code table, written in full, then maps each cell to a word for each instrument (Figure 1).1

3From reading to verdict

The codes of one point form its signature row: needle, needle_pair, sink, flow, across, stability, two_start, probe, and a drift code. The probe column comes from the declared couplings, never from data: it records whether across probes every coupling.1

The rulebook is data. It is a file of rows; each row lists, for some columns, the codes it accepts, and an omitted column accepts every value, nulls included. At load the rulebook is expanded over the whole signature domain and refused unless its rows are pairwise disjoint and together cover the domain. Exactly one row therefore matches any signature, whatever the row order. In words: with the starts independent and the flow stopped, a relevant and stable probed direction gives POINT; marginal and stable gives LINE; relevant or marginal and crossing gives WALKING; an unresolved stability or across gives a refusal. With the starts independent, a slow flow gives LINE only if the probed direction is marginal and stable, and otherwise REFUSE-SLOW. An incomplete or unknown probe turns POINT, LINE or WALKING into a probe refusal.1

Nothing undeclared is inferred, and a null is a value. An absent declaration gives a typed null, NOT-MEASURED or NOT-EVALUATED, with its reason. The null travels to the verdict as a REFUSE-*-UNMEASURED word or a probe refusal; it is never an error.1

Two smaller partitions read the same codes. The geometry axis reads needle, flow, across and stability (POINT, LINE, WALKING, NOT, and refusals). The order axis reads needle, two_start and fluct: COEXIST-AT-WINDOW where the starts sit apart; FIRST-ORDER-TUNNELLING where the starts agree and the energy fluctuation per bond persists with size; CONTINUOUS where it falls; OFF-WINDOW at a sink; and five refusing values. A compose table, total over geometry and order, maps the pair to a verdict; the fluctuation names the order but moves no verdict. At load a check proves that compose equals the per-point verdict on every row of the signature domain.1

A line is a property of many points, so a family layer reads a declared run of points from their stored per-point results and returns LINE, BKT-END, POINT-MANIFOLD, or one of five refusals. It never re-measures, and its rulebook is held to the same disjoint-and-total check.1

4What it refuses to do

The design states its refusals as rules:1

  • No reading without a declaration. A table that a declared instrument needs and that is missing is refused; so is a table that no declared instrument needs. A missing measurement becomes a typed null and then a named refusal.
  • No interval admitted by its own estimate. A rule that decides whether an interval may be used, computed from the estimate it guards, selects for its own failures: a low estimate of the autocorrelation time both passes the floor and narrows the interval. Eligibility is therefore declared, as the number of independent chains or an autocorrelation time, and never read from the series it admits.
  • No cell with the wrong replicate count. A cell without exactly m chains, or a declared rank r other than the method’s, is refused at declaration.
  • No rulebook that leaves a gap or an overlap. One that is not disjoint and total is refused at load.
  • No claim that a line ends from stored data. In the geometry-only line follower, each side oracle declares its evidence class, and the class owns the set of terminal types. The type for finite stored evidence has no END member, so a follower on stored evidence cannot claim that a line ends, by construction of the types rather than by a check.
  • No first-order label from start dependence alone. The Navigator’s start-dependence test never labels a transition first order.

5Fixed inputs

Every input is a declaration file plus hashed data files. The declaration holds fixed tables: the instruments to run and, for each point, its provenance (exact truth, navigation, or observed); the axes (the ordered points of the control path or plane, the strictly ascending sizes, and the starts); the observable; the blocking map; the relations on which differences are taken; the conjugate observable and the system’s couplings, with which are probed; the sink values and the two-start observable; the interval method with its parameters and one margin per quantity read; and the data, with one hashed entry per point, size, start and chain. A verdict is thus a function of the declaration and the data alone.1

Development work is under way. Early pieces are being tried on test problems whose answers are known exactly. Nothing has been shown to work. There is no real-data confirmatory test yet: real data so far have produced only limitation reports and a refusal. The shipped navigation commands are untested, and no line placed by them has been scored against a known one. Results, each with its status, belong in the methods paper.1

The code is private for now. It is to be released as the Python package criticality-compass under the Apache-2.0 licence, alongside the methods paper. The design note is released under CC BY 4.0.1

7Relation to the literature

Early-warning signals. Critical slowing down near a bifurcation shows up in monitoring data as slower recovery from perturbations, rising lag-1 autocorrelation and increasing variance (Scheffer et al. 2012).3 Metric-based and model-based early-warning indicators read these changes in a time series (Dakos et al. 2012).2 A learned classifier assigns a bifurcation type to a time series (Bury et al. 2021).4 The Compass is designed to read critical geometry under size scaling rather than bifurcation type in time series; the design note describes the two as complementary.1

The multivariate direction. The natural fluctuations of a multivariate time series can reveal the direction of lowest resilience, the combination of variables along which recovery is slowest (Weinans et al. 2019).5 A comparison of thirteen multivariate indicators across six scenarios found that no single indicator performs best in all of them (Weinans et al. 2021).6

Criticality across many parameters. In a high-dimensional parameter space the critical points need not be isolated: they can form a critical manifold, along which a system can drift while staying critical and reach multicritical points where it is critical in several ways at once (Gross 2021).7 In an adaptive network model, homeostatic-plasticity-like rules drive the system to the onset of activity and then, while it stays critical, along the critical manifold (Sormunen, Gross & Saramäki 2023).8

Statistical physics. Finite-size scaling estimates one exponent or crossing point (Fisher & Barber 1972);9 block-spin comparison by Monte Carlo is prior work (Ma 1976; Swendsen 1979).10,11 For the variety of universality classes in lattice systems, see Ódor (2004).20

8Glossary

input
A declaration file plus hashed data files. Nothing outside it is read or inferred.
point
One of the ordered points of a declared control path or plane, measured at the declared, strictly ascending sizes.
instrument
One declared measurement (needle, across, stability, two_start, along, fluct, and auxiliary forms of these). Each takes differences of a declared observable across declared relations.
relation
The declared edges on which differences are taken: size pairs, matched blocking levels, a chain of control points, a pair of starts.
reading
An estimate (the median over independent chains) with its order-statistic interval, and the interval’s sign cell: where it sits among the cuts −ε, 0, +ε.
code
A word that a total code table assigns to a sign cell (or to earlier codes); for example SIG+ becomes RELEVANT in the across table.
signature row
The codes of one point: needle, needle_pair, sink, flow, across, stability, two_start, probe, drift code.
rulebook
A data file of rows mapping signature rows to verdicts, refused at load unless its rows are pairwise disjoint and cover every possible signature.
verdict
The word given by the one rulebook row that matches a signature row; each word falls under MC, Not MC or Can’t tell.
typed null
NOT-MEASURED or NOT-EVALUATED with its reason: a value, not an error, that travels to a named refusal.
case
A target model whose answer is known exactly or to high precision, so that a verdict on it is scored right, wrong or refused.
geometry
The partition that reads needle, flow, across and stability only: POINT, LINE, WALKING, NOT, or a refusal.
order
The partition that reads needle, two_start and fluct only: whether the starts coexist at the window, and when they agree, whether the energy fluctuation per bond persists or falls with size. A compose table turns (geometry, order) into the verdict.
