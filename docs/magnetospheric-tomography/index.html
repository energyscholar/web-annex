Convergence Summary

1. FAC Structure

SKT sits at the R1/R2 FAC reversal — a local minimum in |FAC| where the current changes sign. The spatial gradient is maximized here: ground magnetometers see perturbation amplitudes that differ systematically from neighbors. Per-pass ratio: 0.34–2.48, matching ground LISA MLT dependence.

2. Plasmapause

RBSP HOPE shows a 2:1 density drop between L=5.6 (SKT) and L=6.3 (STF). SKT maps to inside the plasmasphere (cold, dense); STF maps to the plasma sheet (hot, tenuous). Temperature jumps 2012→8609 eV. The plasmapause IS the R1/R2 boundary at this L-shell range.

3. Ionospheric Density

Swarm EFI Langmuir probe: N_elec ratio = 1.02. The ionosphere above SKT and STF has identical electron density. Therefore ionospheric conductivity differences do NOT explain the ground perturbation anomaly. The cause is in the magnetospheric current input, not the ionospheric closure path.

4. Oval Boundary

AOB equatorward boundary median = 65.1° QDLat ≈ SKT. Evening-midnight boundary 64.5° is equatorward of SKT. SKT is inside the oval 74% of passes, sub-auroral 23%. Straddling (SKT out, STF in) in 15% — exactly when the ground LISA signature would be strongest.
Conclusion: All persistent LISA hot outliers localize magnetospheric current system boundaries. SKT/STF mark the R1/R2 FAC boundary = plasmapause = equatorward auroral oval edge.
